Privacy Policy.

Last updated · July 24th, 2025

I respect your privacy and aim to keep your personal information safe. This page explains what data may be collected through this website, why it is collected, and how it is handled.

1. What we collect

A. Information you provide

  • Contact details: name, email, and messages you send through forms
  • Project details you share for proposals or collaboration
  • Newsletter signup information, if you subscribe

B. Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages viewed, referring URLs, and basic interaction events
  • Device data: browser type, device type, operating system
  • Log data: IP address, timestamps, and error logs

C. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and similar tools may be used to keep the website working, remember preferences, and measure performance. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

2. How we use your information

  • Respond to inquiries and provide project support
  • Operate and improve the website
  • Send service-related messages you request
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. How we share information

Personal data is not sold. Information may be shared with service providers who help run the website (hosting, analytics, email delivery), or with legal authorities when required by law.

4. Data retention and security

Data is retained only as long as needed for the purposes above, then deleted or anonymized according to retention practices, unless law requires longer storage. Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no system is completely secure.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. Contact hello@winnie.studio to make a request.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page. See also the Terms of Service.
